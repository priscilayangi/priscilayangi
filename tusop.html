The unseen side of pollution

Photography Collection (2024)

Rather than just documenting typical pollution, this photo series aimed to capture multiple aspects of environmental damage and bringing visual awareness to ecological changes that us humans witness daily but choose to ignore.

While working on this project, I found myself seeing analogies between several photographer's series/work. These include Masahisa Fukase's Solitude of Ravens and From Window series. Both groups of work convey a sense of isolation and a melancholy tone regarding the psychological impact of a malfunctioning world. Eamonn Doyle's approach to capturing normal people navigating a poorly degraded urban habitat mirrored the birds' tendency to indulge in the filthy environment around them, and Edward Burtynsky's large-scale industrial landscapes have a strong connection to the project's purpose of displaying plastic's overwhelming global influence.
