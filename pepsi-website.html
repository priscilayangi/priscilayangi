Pepsi Redesign

Website (2026)

I decided to start this project after seeing a video on crystal pepsi, a taste that has been discontinued for some years now. Looking at the video made me want to learn more about Pepsi's previous flavours. To my surprise, I discovered that Pepsi lacked product information and did not fully showcase their brand on their website. That inspired an idea: what if Pepsi redesigned their cans and brought back past flavors as part of a new branding strategy?

Tools Used:

Html, Css, JS, Blender, Illustrator

A lot of bugs came with making this project, lots of crashes with the 3d models, video scroll and carousel. Definitely was a learning experience. Although this was the case it landed me a nomination from Smedias in the category Best Interactive Digital Experience.
