Modula

Typeface (2026)

Originally designed as a component of a t-shirt design, this typeface eventually evolved into a whole typeface that included all of the alphabets, both capital and lowercase, numbers, and the most widely used special characters. The bolded, modular font style is the only feature of this typeface. Modula may have up to five distinct styles in the future: Light, Regular, Medium, Semi-Bold, and Bold.

Tools Used:

Illustrator, FontForge
