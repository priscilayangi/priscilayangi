CLAUSTRA

Interactive Video Installation (2026)

Claustra was an abstract work that aimed to evoke emotions by focusing on themes related to society's excessive use of technology, highlighting our reliance on it for self-expression and maintaining connections. This exhibition was attended by approximately 100 people. In Cyprus, this exhibition was also reinstalled. My primary responsibilities included website mockups, graphic design (branding), and serving as one of the primary visual artists. I had composed a piece for one of my primary projects, the Interactive Hand Tracking Piece, and turned it into a live visual that users could interact with, similarly to Imogen Heap's MiMu gloves.

A lot of prepartion was to be made with this installation, we had a couple of run-ins with equipment, thus the reason for a couple of visual changes. We worked on this project for over a year. Along that time period we learnt a lot about how we could achieve an abstract idea/theme to the public without needing to tell them our idea through words, only actions.
