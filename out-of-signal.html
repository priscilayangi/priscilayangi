OUT OF SIGNAL

Short film (2025)

This storyline revolves around a character who lives in his natural surroundings and is unaware of the city’s beauty as he moves through it. He consistently uses his camera to capture everything, not his own eyes. As he is travelling through the city, he is always capturing moments to share with his friends and followers. Everything around him is fractured; movements are delayed, and reactions are waiting for him to fully witness and feel for himself.

Tools Used:

Premiere Pro, Davinci Resolve
