Documentary Film (2026)

More than a Sound, "What is Indie-Rock"

This documentary follows the journey of a four-man community band who use their music to promote inclusiveness and bring people together. The documentary features personal stories through honest band interviews and behind-the-scenes footage. The film, which incorporates archival video of their musical background, follows the band's identity from the start, showcasing their origins, problems they endured, their first shows, and future objectives.

Group Members:

Priscila Yangi, Christopher Keatinge, Saskia Ludlow-Cederblad

My role consisted of being the main DOP, as well as focusing on the sound (during post) and assisting with post-production editing: colour grading & final edit
effects/finalisations.
