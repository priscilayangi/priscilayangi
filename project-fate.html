PROJECT F.A.T.E

Website Game (2026)

Fallen. Angels. That. Evolve.
Most people’s fate changes depending on who & what they meet, Today, you choose yours.

Project F.A.T.E revolves around a character named Dex Park. He wakes up from what feels like a coma, everything feels like he’s experienced it before, yet they are unfamiliar. He goes off searching the place around him to figure out where he is and why he is in there. As he progressively searches, he slowly finds a path/choice of what he could be doing with his life.

This story operates through interactions with objects, people, and places. As you search, things grow more certain as you get closer to a road that you believe is interesting and your destiny. Some may call it your fate.

Tools Used:

Html, CSS, JS, Clip Stuido Paint, Fl Studio

Creating the sound design was an important component of making the game seem more connected and adding to its ambiance. Small fun fact: All of the sounds you hear, except for the voices, are sound bites of my voice that have been manipulated.
